what it does

Grabs attention.
Explains in a minute.

Instead of stock photos and feature lists, one hand-drawn story carries the whole page. Visitors scroll and watch Luis get sick, use his credits and walk out happy, while the old insurance path plays out beside him in black.

everyone in the process

Simpler for
every actor.

  • Employees: credits on their phone, a local clinic, no claim forms.
  • Business owners: one flat monthly price per employee, with a live team calculator.
  • Doctors: patients from local employers and direct payment, no insurance billing.
how it was made

Built from
your own site.

Every line on the page comes from what you already say on harperhealthnetwork.com, plus public data on US employer insurance. Names, copy and the price are placeholders until we fill them in together.

what comes next

More details,
a bigger story.

Share more with us (real pricing, provider stories, photos of your clinics and your people) and the story grows with you. Your other pages get a calmer, simpler design where Luis becomes your mascot: he walks visitors through the site and brings them to one place, the button to partner with Harper.
